stonecrops

genus Crassula

Member of stonecrop family (family Crassulaceae)
dicots (class Magnoliopsida)
flowering plants (subphylum Angiospermae)

iNaturalist has observed more species in the bay area, but with much fewer observations.

Note that there are multiple genera with the common name, “stonecrops.”

sand pygmyweed
Crassula connata

  • annual
  • leaves generally < 6 mm
  • generally 2 flowers per node
  • flower parts generally in 4s
  • pedicels generally > 1.5 mm

mossy stonecrop
Crassula tillaea

  • annual
  • leaves generally < 6 mm
  • generally 2 flowers per node
  • flower parts generally in 3s
  • pedicels generally < 0.5 mm

aquatic pygmyweed
Crassula aquatica

  • annual
  • leaves generally < 6 mm
  • 1 flower per node or terminal
  • sepals ± ½ length of petals
  • wet areas
 fairy stonecrop
Crassula multicava
fairy stonecrop
Crassula multicava
  • perennial herb
  • leaves generally > 10 mm
  • petiole 5–20 mm
  • petals generally 4, 3–4 mm

jade plant
Crassula ovata

  • perennial shrub
  • leaves generally > 10 mm
  • petiole ± 0
  • petals generally 5, 7–10 mm
  • waif

Toxicity of crassula, jade plant/jade tree (Crassula spp.):
