sci: genus Romneya

The name "matilija" is supposedly Chumash (Native American) in origin, and its pronunciation is a weird mix of English and Spanish conventions: muh-TILL-ih-hah.

==hairy matilija poppy:Romneya trichocalyx
color: white
. petals 6--10 cm long
. leaf 5--10 cm long
. sepals glabrous
. peduncle glabrous

==coulter's matilija poppy:Romneya coulteri
color: white
. petals 4--8 cm long
. leaf 3--10 cm long
. sepals appressed-hairy
. peduncle +- bristly at top
