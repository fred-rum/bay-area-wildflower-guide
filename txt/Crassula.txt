com: stonecrops
sci: genus Crassula
x:iNaturalist has observed more species in the bay area, but with much fewer observations.

Note that there are multiple genera with the common name, "stonecrops."

==sand pygmyweed:C. connata
. annual
. leaves generally < 6 mm
. generally 2 flowers per node
. flower parts generally in 4s
. pedicels generally > 1.5 mm

==mossy stonecrop: C. tillaea
. annual
. leaves generally < 6 mm
. generally 2 flowers per node
. flower parts generally in 3s
. pedicels generally < 0.5 mm

==aquatic pygmyweed:C. aquatica
. annual
. leaves generally < 6 mm
. 1 flower per node or terminal
. sepals +- 1/2 length of petals
. wet areas

==fairy stonecrop:Crassula multicava
color: pale purple
. perennial herb
. leaves generally > 10 mm
. petiole 5--20 mm
. petals generally 4, 3--4 mm

==jade plant:C. ovata
. perennial shrub
. leaves generally > 10 mm
. petiole +- 0
. petals generally 5, 7--10 mm
. waif
